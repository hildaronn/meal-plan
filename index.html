Your meal plan

Checkmarked ingredients are things you listed; the rest go on your grocery list. Uncheck any meal you don't want counted, or shuffle for different picks.

Grocery list

Only ingredients you didn't already list are included, minus oil, salt, and pepper (assumed on hand). When a meal repeats across several days, its quantities are scaled up to cover the whole stretch — look for "(scaled up)". About 87% of ingredients have a real parsed quantity; the rest show just the ingredient name, so double-check those by eye.

Cooking plan

Every recipe in your plan, grouped into a batch-cooking order — do all your chopping and prep first, get the oven going, use the stovetop while the oven works, then finish everything up. Steps are sorted by keyword (oven/stovetop/prep/finish), not verified timing, so adjust to your own kitchen and equipment.

Recipes come from a public recipe dataset (Epicurious recipes, CC BY-SA 3.0, via josephrmartinez/recipe-dataset on GitHub) matched automatically against your ingredients — titles and instructions are as originally written, but ingredient categories, meal-type tags, and nutrition figures are generated with simple heuristics, not verified by a person. Use your judgment, especially around food safety and allergies. The photo feature sends your image directly to Anthropic's API using an API key you provide; that key is stored only in your browser and this site never sees or stores it.
